Keep a useful phrase close

Free resources for real work moments.

Use these short reference guides to prepare for emails, meetings, and interviews. Each phrase is a starting point—adapt it to your own voice and situation.

✉Email guide

Clearer work emails

Polite phrases for common email moments.

  • Could you clarify what you need from me by Thursday?Ask for a clearer request and include a useful deadline.
  • I’m following up on the proposal I sent yesterday.Bring a previous message back into the conversation.
  • Would you be able to share the latest version?Make a direct request with a courteous tone.

↔Meeting guide

Move a meeting forward

Useful ways to contribute and make the next step clear.

  • Could I add one point before we move on?Join the conversation without interrupting abruptly.
  • Let’s align on the next steps before Friday.Check that everyone shares the same plan.
  • Who will take the lead on this action?Confirm ownership so the follow-up is clear.

◇Interview guide

Talk about your experience

Prompts to make your examples specific and easy to follow.

  • One example that comes to mind is…Move from a general claim into a concrete story.
  • My role was to coordinate the launch.Explain your own contribution to a team result.
  • The outcome was a shorter handover time.Close with a clear, relevant result.
