align

/əˈlaɪn/ verb

Plain definition

To agree on a plan or make sure people and activities are working toward the same goal.

“ In a work conversation

“Before we share the proposal with the client, let’s align on the timeline and who owns each next step.”

Use it naturally

Common ways to say it

These combinations are useful when teams need to get on the same page.

  • align on a plan
  • align with the team
  • align expectations
  • get aligned

Helpful pattern: use “align on” for a shared decision, and “align with” for a person, goal, or set of priorities.
