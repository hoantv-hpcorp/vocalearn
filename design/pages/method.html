Useful, not overwhelming

Four simple steps, at your pace.

Each step connects learning to the situations you meet in a real workday.

  1. STEP 01 ⌕

    Choose work-relevant words

    Start with a situation you care about—like writing a clear update, joining a meeting, or preparing for an interview.

  2. STEP 02 “

    Learn with context

    See what a word means and how it can sound in a sentence, so it feels connected to a moment you might actually face.

  3. STEP 03 ↻

    Review at the right time

    Short reviews bring words back for another look. Keep the pace manageable and revisit what needs more practice.

  4. STEP 04 ↗

    Use them with confidence

    Try a new word in a message or conversation when it fits. Make it your own, and keep learning as your work changes.

A word in its element

Meaning is clearer in a moment you recognize.

Rather than meeting a word alone, connect it with a short, everyday work example. Then think of where you might use it.
