The essentials

Here’s what people often ask.

Still wondering about something? We’re happy to hear from you.

How much time do I need?

WordStack is designed for short sessions—about five minutes a day. Go at your own pace; a missed day is no reason to feel behind.

Does it cost anything to get started?

You can start for free. Visit Start here to try a short level check and explore a learning path.

Do I need to install an app?

No app installation is needed to explore WordStack. You can use the site in a web browser, including on your phone.

Is WordStack suitable if I’m a beginner?

Yes. You can begin from your current level and take things one step at a time. The goal is useful progress, not perfect English.

Why learn words in context instead of from a list?

A word is easier to understand when you can see how it works in a real situation. WordStack connects practical workplace words with examples, rather than asking you to rely on a list alone. Explore the learning method or browse word packs.

Where can I read reviews?

We don’t use made-up ratings or quotes. If you have a question about learner feedback, get in touch.

How does WordStack handle personal data?

We don’t want to guess or promise details that aren’t explained here. If you have questions about how information is handled, please contact us before sharing anything you consider sensitive.

How can I contact WordStack?

Send us a note through the contact page. We welcome questions, feedback, and suggestions.

Didn’t find your answer?

Send us your question and we’ll be glad to help.
