Privacy and terms.

Last updated 28 October 2026

About this document. This is a prototype draft written to be read, not skimmed. It follows the spirit of PDPA (Singapore), APPI (Japan), and PIPL (China): collect little, explain why, and let people delete what they shared.

Privacy policy

WordStack exists to help you learn words. To do that, it needs to remember a small amount about your progress — and almost nothing else.

What we store

  • Your email address, so you can sign in and recover your account.
  • Your level check result and the packs you have started.
  • Your review history: which words you saw, when, and how you graded them.
  • Words you save to My Words, and your first-language preference for glosses.
  • An approximate region, such as “Singapore”, from your browser settings — not your precise location.

Why we store it

Your review history is the product. Spaced repetition only works if the site knows when a word is due. Saved words and pack progress let you pick up where you left off. We do not build advertising profiles or sell data to third parties.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell personal data.
  • We do not use third-party advertising trackers.
  • We do not ask for your phone number, address, or employer.
  • We do not send marketing email unless you opt in separately.

Your choices

You can export your saved words and review history from the Progress page, and you can delete your account at any time. Deleting an account removes its learning data within 30 days; encrypted backups may take up to 90 days to expire. You can use the free starter pack without creating an account.

Retention and security

Active accounts keep their data until deletion. Data is transmitted over HTTPS and stored on servers in Singapore. Access is limited to the people who run the service, and we keep an audit log of account access.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@wordstack.example. If your local law gives you a specific right — access, correction, portability, or objection — say so in the message and we will respond within 30 days.

Terms of use

By using WordStack, you agree to these terms. They are written to be understood, not to hide anything in the footnotes.

The service

WordStack offers a free tier with the level check, one starter pack, daily review, and progress tracking. Paid packs, if introduced, add more situations and words. Prices shown during the prototype are illustrative and will be confirmed before any charge.

Your account

Keep one account per person and keep your sign-in details safe. Tell us promptly if you think someone else has access. You are responsible for activity under your account.

Content and use

Word packs, explanations, and example sentences are for your personal learning. Do not resell them, publish them as your own, or scrape the site to build a competing product. Short quotations for teaching or review are fine.

Availability and results

We aim to keep the site available and your progress safe, but we cannot promise uninterrupted service. WordStack is a learning tool, not a guarantee of a test score, a job offer, or a promotion.

Changes

If these terms change in a meaningful way, we will show a notice in the product before the change takes effect. Continuing to use WordStack after that means you accept the updated terms.

Contact and governing law

Questions about these terms: hello@wordstack.example. These terms are governed by the laws of Singapore, without regard to conflict-of-law rules.
