Inside the pack

A preview of seven words from this pack. Each one has a plain-English meaning, a workplace example, and audio.

leverage

verb

To use something you already have to get a better result.

pushback

noun

Disagreement or resistance to a plan or idea.

align

verb

To reach agreement on a plan, priority, or next step.

circle back

phrase

To return to a topic later, usually after getting more information.

bandwidth

noun

The time and energy someone has available for work.

stakeholder

noun

Anyone affected by or interested in a project or decision.

deliverable

noun

A concrete result you promise to produce for a project.
