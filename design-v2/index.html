Questions

A few things you might be wondering.

How much time does it really take?

Five minutes, by design. Sessions are timed and capped, so you always know where the end is. Some days you’ll do nine cards; some days three.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free tier is free forever and includes the level check, a starter pack, and daily review. Paid packs, if they come, add more situations — not access to your own progress.

Do I need to install an app?

No. WordStack works in your phone’s browser. Add it to your home screen if you want it to feel like an app, but there’s nothing to download.

Will it work for my level?

The two-minute check places you from foundation to upper intermediate and recommends a starting pack. The review schedule adjusts as you go.

What makes this different from a word list?

Context and timing. Words are grouped by the situation you need them in, and spaced repetition brings them back just before they would slip. A list gives you words; this gives you a loop.

Is my data safe?

We keep the minimum needed to remember your progress. The privacy policy is written to be read, and it follows PDPA/APPI/PIPL-aware practices for our region. Read the privacy policy.
